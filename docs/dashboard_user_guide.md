# Dashboard User Guide

## Start the Dashboard

From the repository root, install dependencies and start Streamlit:

```powershell
.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m pip install -r requirements.txt
.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m streamlit run streamlit_app.py
```

The app expects `data/academic_analytics.db` and the KPI views to exist. If the dashboard reports that the database or a KPI view is unavailable, run the established pipeline first:

```powershell
.venv\Scripts\python.exe python\src\generate_synthetic_data.py
.venv\Scripts\python.exe python\src\load_to_sqlite.py
.venv\Scripts\python.exe python\src\calculate_risk.py
.venv\Scripts\python.exe python\src\kpi_service.py --install-views --validate
```

## Navigation and Filters

Use the Dashboard selector in the left sidebar to move among the five pages. The latest available term is selected initially. Department, program, risk level, and risk model filters apply to the active cohort throughout the dashboard. Program choices narrow when a department is selected.

The Executive Overview trend chart always spans available terms. It follows the department, program, risk-level, and risk-model filters, while its KPI cards and risk distribution follow the selected term as well.

The Executive Overview also shows a descriptive academic health index. It is an equal-weighted composite of normalized semester GPA, attendance, inverse failure rate, and inverse high-risk share among assessed students. It is not a validated institutional benchmark.

Choose **Not assessed** in Risk level to inspect students who have no risk assessment for the selected model. These students remain part of the total cohort denominator. Switch the Risk model control to compare `rules-v1` with `source-v1` where source assessments are available.

## Pages

### Executive Overview

The seven KPI cards report cohort size, high/medium/low risk counts, average semester GPA, average attendance, and failure rate. The risk distribution includes students without an assessment. The trend chart shows risk counts by term, which should be interpreted alongside cohort size.

The executive readout uses the selected cohort to report high-risk exposure, attendance against the existing 75% monitoring threshold, and the most common stored reason among high-risk students. Leadership actions are conditional prompts, not validated intervention prescriptions. Risk reasons are not causal explanations.

### Academic Risk Analysis

The histogram shows the distribution of numeric risk scores. The breakdown shows category counts, including unassessed students. Top risk factors count distinct selected-cohort students mentioning each stored reason. Student ranking sorts assessed students by score. The high-risk table includes student ID, department, program, score, and stored reasons.

Risk reasons are generated by the existing rules engine. They describe recorded warning signals and should not be read as causal findings or as a substitute for advising review.

### Department Analytics

Risk bars compare category counts by department. GPA and attendance bars show academic performance and participation. The heatmap standardizes each metric inside the filtered cohort; hover to see the actual metric value. Color indicates relative deviation from the cohort mean, not an absolute target or grade. The highest-risk table ranks by high-risk share among assessed students and shows counts for workload context. The best-performing table ranks by the dashboard's descriptive health index.

### Program Analytics

High-risk share ranks programs by high-risk students divided by assessed students. The scatter plot compares average semester GPA and attendance; bubble size indicates program cohort size and color indicates high-risk share. The table provides the underlying values.

### Student Drill Down

Select a student from the filtered term cohort. The profile includes academic standing, GPA, attendance, enrollment and failure counts, risk score/category, recorded reasons, failed course details, and available GPA and risk-score histories. Risk chart background bands mark the existing Low (<30), Medium (30–<60), and High (>=60) thresholds. A missing score means the selected model has no assessment for that student-term.

## KPI Definitions and Sources

| KPI | Meaning | Warehouse source |
|---|---|---|
| Total students | Distinct enrolled students in the selected term and filters | `vw_kpi_student_term` |
| High/medium/low risk students | Distinct students with the selected model's latest category | `vw_kpi_latest_risk_assessment` through `vw_kpi_student_term` |
| Average GPA | Mean semester GPA among records with a GPA value | `fact_gpa_history` through the KPI view |
| Attendance rate | Mean student-term attendance rate; source attendance is lecture-weighted in the view | `fact_attendance` through the KPI view |
| Failure rate | Failed course-final records divided by completed course-final records | `fact_grade` and `fact_enrollment` through the KPI view |
| Risk score/category/reasons | Latest assessment for the selected model and term | `fact_risk_assessment` through KPI views |
| Department/program metrics | Student-term records attributed to their term enrollment program and department | `dim_program`, `dim_department`, and KPI view |

Risk share uses assessed students as its denominator. Failure rate excludes withdrawn courses and records without a valid course-final grade according to the KPI view. Students without a risk assessment remain in total cohort counts but not in risk-share denominators.

## Refresh and Limitations

Dashboard data is cached for five minutes. Clear Streamlit's cache after an ETL reload if updated data is needed immediately. The dashboard is read-only and does not create KPI views or run risk scoring.

The warehouse data is synthetic. Scores, risk reasons, and comparisons are for analytics demonstration; they are not student diagnoses, causal claims, or intervention decisions. Filtered comparisons can become small, so review cohort counts alongside percentages.
